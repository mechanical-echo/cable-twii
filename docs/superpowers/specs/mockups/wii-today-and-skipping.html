Wii Today list and the Today pill

Mockup for the Today list and skipping on looping channels, drawn in the Wii redesign style before anything is built. Every frame is the app's 640×480 picture at 1:1, using the tokens from wii/source/ui/theme.h, the app's fonts and pointer, and the real OSD panel and guide geometry from osd_layout.c and guide_layout.c. Shows and titles are made up.
