Wii Settings screen, keyboard and the new Settings pills

Mockup for plan 08, Task 8: the screens that let a Wii play from a cable-twii server, drawn in the Wii redesign style before anything is built. Every frame is the app's 640×480 picture at 1:1, drawn with the tokens from wii/source/ui/theme.h, the real wordmark, glyphs and pointer from wii/data/, and the app's text placement (em-box top, pill labels centred, labels shortened with "…" or wrapped exactly as ui_text_fit / ui_text_wrap do). The numbers at the bottom are what Tasks 9 and 11 will build.

Approved pixel numbers

Approved by the owner on 2026-11-19. Rects are x, y, w, h in the 640×480 frame. For text lines, y is the em-box top (what ui_text takes), w is the width the text is shortened or wrapped to, and h is the font size, or lines × line height. Where a rect is centred, the value is computed the same way the frames are.

About the second column: the brief asked for pixel_x 1.0 and 1.333, but the app's ui_pixel_x() is 0.75 on 16:9 (asp_pixel_x: (640/480)/(16/9)). 1.333 is how much a 16:9 TV stretches each pixel; parts that must look square are drawn 0.75× as wide to undo it. So the second column is pixel_x 0.75. Like every pill in the app today, pills, keys and fields keep their widths on both TVs, so most rows are the same in both columns. Rows that differ are highlighted.
